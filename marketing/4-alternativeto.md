## Description
StreamPulse is the ultimate Chrome extension for Twitch and Kick viewers. While most extensions focus on adding emotes and modifying the chat (like BetterTTV or FrankerFaceZ), StreamPulse is built purely to automate your viewing experience without adding visual bloat to your browser.

If you often leave streams open in a background tab, StreamPulse does the heavy lifting for you:

Key Features:
- Channel Points Auto-Claim: Never miss a bonus chest on Twitch again. It clicks automatically, even when you're in another tab.
- Twitch Drops Auto-Claim: Secures your Drops inventory items the second they reach 100%.
- Cross-Platform Live Notifications: Get instant, lightweight desktop alerts when a streamer goes live on either Twitch or Kick.
- Error 2000 / 3000 Fixer: Automatically forces the video player to recover from network errors without refreshing the entire page.
- Advanced Chat Filters: Keep your chat clean by blocking spam, bots, or specific keywords.
- Anti-Pause Feature: Stops Twitch from automatically pausing streams when the tab loses focus.

StreamPulse is 100% free, open-source friendly, requires no account creation, and processes everything locally to guarantee absolute privacy. It runs perfectly alongside your existing emote extensions!
