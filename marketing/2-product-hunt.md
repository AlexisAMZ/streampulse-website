# StreamPulse — Twitch & Kick Chrome Extension (Product Hunt)

## 📌 Tagline (Catchphrase)
The ultimate viewer extension to automate Twitch Drops & Channel Points.

## 📝 Description courte (Listings)
StreamPulse automates repetitive tasks for Twitch and Kick viewers: auto-claim Channel Points and Drops, real-time live notifications, chat filters, and automatic error #2000 fixing. Lightweight, free, and fully private.

## 💬 Maker's Comment (Premier commentaire à poster sur la page)
Hey Product Hunt! 👋 I'm AlexisAMZ, the creator of StreamPulse.

I built this extension because I was tired of missing out on Twitch Channel Points and Drops just because I had the stream running in a background tab or forgot to click the chest icon. 

While tools like BetterTTV are amazing for chat customization, I wanted something purely focused on automating the *viewer experience* across both **Twitch** and **Kick**.

**✨ Core Features:**
- 🎁 **Auto-Claim:** Channel Points and Drops are instantly claimed in the background.
- 🔔 **Cross-Platform Live Alerts:** Get notified when your favorite streamer goes live on Twitch or Kick.
- 🛠 **Player Fixer:** Automatically reloads the player when Twitch throws a #2000 or #3000 error.
- 🚫 **Anti-Pause:** Prevents Twitch from pausing streams in inactive tabs.

StreamPulse is 100% free, requires no account, and doesn't collect any personal data. It plays perfectly alongside your existing emote extensions. 

I’d love to hear your feedback, and I'm here to answer any questions! Let me know what features you'd like to see next. 🚀
