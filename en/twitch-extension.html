Twitch extension for viewers

StreamPulse is a free browser extension that automates the Twitch viewer experience: auto-claim Channel Points, Drops, real-time notifications, chat filters, and automatic player error fixing. Compatible with Chrome, Edge, Brave and Opera.

All Twitch features

StreamPulse focuses on automation and tracking for Twitch viewers. Unlike emote extensions, StreamPulse does not change chat appearance but automates repetitive tasks.

  • Auto-claim Channel Points. Automatic collection of Twitch channel points, even in the background. No more clicking the bonus manually.
  • Automatic Twitch Drops. Instant detection and claiming of Drops as soon as conditions are met.
  • Live notifications. Real-time desktop alerts whenever a followed streamer goes live on Twitch.
  • Player error fixing. Automatic stream reload during #1000, #2000, or #3000 errors. No more manual interruptions.
  • Chat filters. Hide spam, unwanted keywords, and repetitive messages in Twitch chat.
  • Anti-pause. Prevents Twitch from pausing the video when the tab goes to the background.
  • Hover video previews. Preview live streams by hovering over a channel link, without leaving your page.

Browser compatibility

StreamPulse installs from the Chrome Web Store and works on all Chromium-based browsers: Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, and Vivaldi. No extra setup is needed.

StreamPulse and BetterTTV: complementary

StreamPulse and BetterTTV do not do the same thing. BetterTTV (and FrankerFaceZ, 7TV) add emotes and customize chat. StreamPulse automates viewer tasks. Both can be installed together without conflicts.

If you want an emote extension, install BetterTTV or FrankerFaceZ. If you want an extension to automate Channel Points, Drops, and notifications, install StreamPulse. Both combined cover all the needs of a regular Twitch viewer.

Privacy

StreamPulse collects no personal data. No trackers, no ads, no account required. All preferences are stored locally in your browser.

FAQ

Is StreamPulse free?

Yes, 100% free, no ads, no paid features, and no account required.

Does StreamPulse auto-claim Channel Points?

Yes. StreamPulse detects and clicks the Channel Points bonus as soon as it appears, even if the tab is in the background.

Does StreamPulse auto-claim Twitch Drops?

Yes. StreamPulse detects available Drops and claims them automatically once conditions are met.

Do Channel Points work in the background?

Yes. StreamPulse's anti-pause keeps the stream active even when the tab is not in the foreground, allowing continuous Channel Points collection.

Who develops StreamPulse?

StreamPulse is developed by AlexisAMZ, an independent developer. The source code and updates are available on GitHub.
